About the author

A library
of his own.

Axel Macedo is a Houston-born creative and author building a body of work one story at a time. This site is designed to feel less like a résumé and more like a real literary room — quiet, tactile, personal, and made for discovering books.

As the collection grows, each book can become a destination of its own: cover art, synopsis, excerpts, editions, reviews, and a direct path to purchase.
